# LinkedIn

In May I wrote a post about recording a podcast that ended with "the episode lands at some point later". It landed.

Laco Šulák and I recorded an episode of The Tech Roast, Absa's podcast. Four topics: where AI actually fits into an engineering process, event-driven architecture on AWS, container orchestration, and async patterns in service communication.

None of it was scripted. We agreed the areas beforehand, wrote down the positions we each held, noted where we knew we disagreed, and left everything past that to the conversation.

That was the part worth getting right. A rehearsed answer sounds rehearsed, and it removes the room you need to respond when the person across from you pushes back.

We finished on where AI goes next, which is the part that will age fastest.
