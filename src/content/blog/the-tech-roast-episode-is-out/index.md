Back in May I [wrote about recording an episode](/blog/the-tech-roast-ai-event-driven-aws-and-async-patterns/)
of The Tech Roast, Absa's podcast, with Laco Šulák. That post ended with the
episode landing "at some point later". It has landed:
[watch it here](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7493442524913565696/).

We covered four things — where AI actually fits into an engineering process,
event-driven architecture on AWS, container orchestration, and async patterns in
service communication — and finished on where AI goes next, which is the part
that will age fastest.

None of it was scripted. We agreed the areas beforehand, wrote down the
positions we each held, noted where we knew we disagreed, and left the rest to
the conversation. If you want the longer version of how it was put together and
what a studio day looks like from an engineer's side, that is in
[the earlier post](/blog/the-tech-roast-ai-event-driven-aws-and-async-patterns/).

Thanks to Laco, who made the conversation easy, and to the crew who did the
lighting and sound work that none of you will notice.
